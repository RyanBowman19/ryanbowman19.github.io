    Straight answers

    Common questions

    What does a website cost?

    Use the estimator above for a ballpark. You'll receive a fixed price before any work begins, and it doesn't change unless you request additional work.

    Is my business too small?

    No. One-person businesses are most of what I work with.

    Smaller businesses are routinely treated as smaller accounts: quoted out of range, passed around, or sold work they didn't need. They're exactly who this is for.

    What if I don't like what you build?

    You'll see the site as it's built rather than at the end, and two rounds of revisions are included, so there's room to steer it.

    We also agree on direction before I start, which resolves most of it in advance.

    How long does it take?

    A straightforward site takes one to two weeks. A larger one, three to four. The usual delay is waiting on photos and details.

    I don't have photos or know what to write.

    That's common. I'll interview you for about half an hour and write the content from your answers. You review and approve it.

    For photos, I can show you how to take usable ones on a phone, or source stock that doesn't look like stock.

    Do I own it, or am I renting?

    You own it. The domain is registered in your name and the files are yours. If you move on, you take everything with you.

    No rebuild fee, and no withholding your site. It's a common practice in this industry and not one I follow.

    Can I change it myself?

    Yes. Anything you'd update regularly, such as hours, prices, or events, can be edited without touching code. I'll walk you through it and record the walkthrough so you can rewatch it.

    Will Google find me?

    Every site is built for it: proper page titles, descriptions, a sitemap, and clean markup search engines can read.

    Rankings take time, and anyone guaranteeing you a position isn't being straight with you.

    What if I mostly like my current site?

    Then we keep it. A targeted update often costs a fraction of a rebuild and delivers most of the benefit. The free review will tell you which you need.

    Where are you, and does it matter?

    I'm based in Indiana, in the US, though location isn't a constraint. I work with clients anywhere.

    Most projects run over email and a couple of calls. If we're in different time zones, I'll work around yours.
