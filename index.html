    Straight answers

    Questions I get a lot

    What does a website cost?

    Use the slider above for a ballpark. You get one fixed price before I start. It doesn't move unless you ask for more.

    I'm really small. Am I too small?

    No. One-person shops are most of what I do.

    Small businesses get treated like small accounts everywhere else — priced out, passed around, or sold something they didn't need. You're the whole reason this exists.

    How long does it take?

    Simple site: one to two weeks. Bigger one: three or four. The hold-up is almost always photos and details from you.

    I don't have photos or know what to write.

    That's normal. I'll ask you questions for half an hour and write the whole thing. You read it and say yes.

    Photos are easier than you think. I'll show you how to shoot usable ones on your phone, or find stock that doesn't look like stock.

    Do I own it, or am I renting?

    You own it. The domain goes in your name and the files are yours. If you ever leave, you take it all.

    No rebuild fee. No holding your site hostage. Some folks in this business do that. I won't.

    Can I change it myself?

    Yes. Anything you'd change often — hours, prices, events — you can edit without touching code. I'll show you how and record it so you can watch again.

    Will Google find me?

    Every site I build is set up for it. Proper page titles, descriptions, a sitemap, and clean code search engines can actually read.

    Rankings take time. Anyone who promises you a spot is lying to you.

    I kind of like my site.

    Then let's keep it. A tune-up often costs a fraction of a rebuild and gets you most of the win. The free review tells you which one you need.

    Where are you, and does it matter?

    I'm based in Indiana, in the US. It doesn't matter where you are — I work with people anywhere.

    Most projects run on email and a couple of calls. If we're in different time zones, I'll work around yours.
